EARLY DEVELOPMENT

NABU

Execution-signal detection for market compliance

NABU is a deterministic, three-layer signal detection engine for trading compliance teams — correlating execution behaviour (order timing, cancellation ratios, venue routing), price-pattern structure, and market/world context to flag spoofing, dark-pool routing, and other anomalous execution patterns. Every flag is traceable to a named threshold; NABU never instructs, it observes and escalates for human compliance review.

Early development. Not a certified surveillance product; not independently audited.
