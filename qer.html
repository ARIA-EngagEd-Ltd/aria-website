QER prices Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later exposure for cyber insurers: the risk that encrypted data intercepted today is decrypted once a quantum computer exists. It is a competing-risks model built around three clocks, not a black box.

Three clocks, one race

An insured's data is exposed only if a quantum computer arrives before the insured finishes migrating to quantum-safe cryptography, and before the data's retention window closes. Move the migration-maturity control below; the model recalculates live, in your browser.

Where this actually stands

Every input above is either sourced to a named, checkable reference, or explicitly labeled an unvalidated placeholder: it is not claimed to be more certain than it is. What QER cannot do yet is validate against real losses, because no Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later claim has been paid anywhere in the market to validate against. That is the gap a design partner closes.
